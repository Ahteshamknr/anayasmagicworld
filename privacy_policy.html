Privacy Policy

App: Anaya's Magic World • Developer: Ahtesham Tech • Effective Date: October 25, 2026

Welcome to Anaya's Magic World ("the App"), developed and maintained by Ahtesham Tech ("we", "our", or "us"). We are committed to protecting your privacy and ensuring a transparent, secure gaming experience for players of all ages.

1. Information We Collect and Process

The App is built with privacy-first principles. We do not require you to create an account, log in, or provide personal details such as your name, physical address, or phone number.

🎤 Microphone Permission Notice (Voice Scream Mechanic):
The App requests access to your device microphone strictly to support the optional in-game voice activation feature (where sound intensity controls character running). Audio data is analyzed solely in volatile local memory in real time to calculate volume levels. We NEVER record, save, store, transmit, or share your voice or audio recordings over the internet. Players can also choose to play using the standard on-screen touch controls at any time without enabling the microphone.

2. Third-Party Services & Advertising

To support ongoing development and offer the App for free, we utilize trusted industry-standard third-party SDKs that may process non-sensitive device identifiers:

  • Google AdMob (Google LLC): Used to serve interstitial and rewarded video ads. AdMob may collect and process device identifiers, including your Google Advertising ID (AAID), IP address, and general device telemetry to manage ad frequency, prevent fraud, and serve advertisements. For more information, please visit Google Advertising Privacy Policy.
  • Google Play In-App Billing: If you purchase in-app items (such as "Remove Ads" or cosmetic bundles), transactions are processed directly by the Google Play Store. We never receive or store your credit card or financial credentials.

3. Advertising ID (AAID)

In compliance with Google Play Developer Program policies, the App uses the Google Play Advertising ID strictly for advertising and analytics purposes as provided by the Google Mobile Ads SDK. Users can reset or opt out of personalized ads at any time via their Android device settings: Settings > Google > Ads > Delete advertising ID or Opt out of Ads Personalization.

4. Data Storage & Security

Your game progress, unlocked outfits, high scores, and sound sensitivity preferences are stored locally on your device using Android encrypted DataStore. All network communications initiated by third-party SDKs (AdMob and Google Play Billing) are encrypted using industry-standard Transport Layer Security (TLS/HTTPS).

5. Children's Privacy

Our game is designed to be joyful, family-friendly, and safe. We do not intentionally solicit or collect personally identifiable information from children under the age of 13. If you are a parent or guardian and believe that your child has provided us with personal information, please contact us immediately, and we will take prompt steps to delete any such data.

6. Your Data Rights & Deletion

Because all game progression data is saved locally on your device, you have complete control over it. You can erase all game data at any time by uninstalling the application or by clearing the App's data via your device settings: Settings > Apps > Anaya's Magic World > Storage > Clear Storage / Clear Data.

7. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes in our practices or applicable legal regulations. Any updates will be posted on this page with an updated "Effective Date."
